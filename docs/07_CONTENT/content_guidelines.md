# Content & Copy Guidelines — Klear Karma v2

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Attributes
| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Warm** | Empathetic, human, never clinical | "We're here to help you find the right fit" |
| **Clear** | Direct, no jargon, no mysticism | "Book a session" not "Begin your journey" |
| **Trustworthy** | Factual, specific, evidence of verification | "47 verified reviews" not "Highly rated" |
| **Grounded** | Practical, not preachy | "30-minute consultation" not "Transform your life" |

### Banned Language
| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do Instead |
|----------|--------------|
| "Transform your life" | "Find what works for you" |
| "Ancient wisdom meets modern science" | "Verified practitioners near you" |
| "Unleash your potential" | "Book your first session" |
| "Scientifically validated" | "Community reviewed" |
| "Biofield" / "Aura" / "Chakra alignment" | "Energy work" / "Reiki" / "Meditation" |
| "Seamless experience" | "Book in 3 taps" |
| "Next-generation platform" | "Simple, verified, trusted" |

## In-App Copy

### Onboarding Screens
1. **Welcome**: "Find healers you can trust. Browse verified practitioners, read real reviews, and book sessions that fit your life."
2. **Preferences**: "Tell us what you're looking for. We'll personalize your recommendations."
3. **Location**: "See practitioners near you. We'll show distance and availability."

### Empty States
- No search results: "No practitioners found for this search. Try broadening your filters or checking a different area."
- No bookings: "You haven't booked a session yet. Browse practitioners to get started."
- No reviews: "Be the first to share your experience."

### Error States
- Network: "Can't connect right now. Check your connection and try again."
- Payment failed: "Payment didn't go through. Please try again or use a different method."
- Slot taken: "This slot was just booked by someone else. Choose another time."

### Push Notifications
| Event | Copy | Timing |
|-------|------|--------|
| Booking confirmed | "Your session with [Name] is confirmed for [Date] at [Time]" | Immediate |
| 24h reminder | "Reminder: You have a session with [Name] tomorrow at [Time]" | -24h |
| 1h reminder | "Starting soon: [Name] in 1 hour" | -1h |
| Session completed | "How was your session with [Name]? Leave a review." | +2h |
| New message | "[Name] sent you a message" | Immediate |
| Practitioner: new booking | "New booking! [Seeker] booked [Date] at [Time]" | Immediate |

### CTA Buttons
- Primary: "Book Session", "Confirm Booking", "Pay ₹[Amount]"
- Secondary: "View Profile", "See Reviews", "Change Filters"
- Tertiary: "Skip", "Maybe Later", "Not Now"

## Email Templates

### Transactional
1. **Welcome** — "Welcome to Klear Karma. Here's how to find your first practitioner."
2. **Booking confirmation** — Summary card: practitioner, date, time, amount, location/online
3. **Payment receipt** — Amount, date, booking ID, Razorpay reference
4. **Review request** — "Your session was [X days] ago. Share your experience in 30 seconds."

### Practitioner Onboarding
1. **Application received** — "We're reviewing your profile. You'll hear back within 48 hours."
2. **Approved** — "You're verified! Complete your profile to start receiving bookings."
3. **First booking** — "Congratulations! [Seeker] just booked a session with you."
