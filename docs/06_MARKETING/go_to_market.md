# Go-to-Market Strategy — Klear Karma v2

## Launch Strategy: Supply-First in Bangalore

### Why Supply-First?
A marketplace with no practitioners is worthless to seekers. We onboard practitioners first, then attract seekers to a platform that already has supply.

### Phase 0: Pre-Launch (4 weeks before)

**Practitioner Onboarding (Target: 50 practitioners)**

| Channel | Approach | Target |
|---------|----------|:---:|
| WhatsApp groups | Healing practitioner communities in Bangalore | 20 |
| Instagram DMs | Active practitioners with 500+ followers | 15 |
| Yoga studio partnerships | Commission-free first 3 months in exchange for exclusivity | 10 |
| Personal network | Direct referrals from founder connections | 5 |

**Messaging for practitioners:**
> "Get a professional booking page, accept payments, and build your reputation — free for the first 3 months. We only earn when you earn."

### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Week 1-4)

**Seeker Acquisition (Target: 100 seekers)**

| Channel | Tactic | CAC Target |
|---------|--------|:---:|
| Instagram (organic) | Behind-the-scenes content: "Meet our verified healers" | ₹0 |
| Google Maps reviews | Encourage practitioners to link Klear Karma from their Maps listing | ₹0 |
| WhatsApp referral | ₹200 credit for both referrer and referee | ₹200 |
| Instagram ads (Bangalore geo) | "Find verified healers near you" | ₹150-250 |

### Phase 2: Growth (Month 2-6)

| Channel | Budget/Month | Expected Seekers/Month |
|---------|:---:|:---:|
| Instagram ads | ₹30K | 150 |
| Google Ads (search: "reiki bangalore", "yoga teacher near me") | ₹20K | 100 |

| **Total** | **₹65K** | **400** |

## Content Strategy (Organic)

### Instagram (primary channel)
- 3-4 posts/week
- Content pillars: Practitioner spotlights, modality education, seeker testimonials, booking tips
- NO: Pseudoscience claims, miracle cure language, "biofield" content

### Blog (SEO play)
- Target: "best [modality] in [city]" long-tail keywords
- 2 posts/month
- Examples: "7 Questions to Ask Before Your First Reiki Session", "How to Choose a Sound Healer in Bangalore"

### WhatsApp Community
- Exclusive group for verified practitioners
- Weekly tips, platform updates, cross-referral opportunities

## What We're NOT Doing

- No paid influencer campaigns until PMF
- No "launch event" or PR until Month 3+
- No multi-city until Bangalore is working
- No B2B / corporate until consumer is proven
- No app install campaigns (organic + referral only initially)
