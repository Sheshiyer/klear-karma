# Pitch Summary — Klear Karma v2

## One-Liner
Community-verified marketplace for alternative healing in India.

---

## The Problem

500,000+ alternative healing practitioners in India have zero digital booking infrastructure. Seekers rely on WhatsApp forwards and Instagram DMs to find practitioners — with no way to verify credentials, read honest reviews, or book/pay securely.

**Current seeker experience:**
1. Ask friends for recommendations
2. Find practitioner on Instagram
3. DM to ask about availability
4. Negotiate price over WhatsApp
5. Pay cash or UPI manually
6. No recourse if session is bad

---

## The Solution

Klear Karma is the **Practo for alternative healing** — a mobile marketplace where:
- Seekers discover, verify, book, and pay practitioners in 3 taps
- Practitioners get a professional presence, booking system, and client pipeline
- Trust is built through multi-layer verification + community reviews

## Business Model

- **Commission**: 20-30% per booking (paid by practitioner)
- **No seeker fees** — seekers pay session price only
- **Practitioner subscription** (Phase 2): ₹2K/mo for premium features
- **Unit economics**: ₹435 gross margin per ₹2,000 booking
